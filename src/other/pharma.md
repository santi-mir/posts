# AI in Pharma

The paper [Enhancing preclinical drug discovery with artificial intelligence][AI_in_pharma] discusses all the areas in which Artificial Intelligence models can improve the Drug Discovery Pipeline.

The steps in which AI can help include:

1. Target Identification,
2. Hit Identification,
3. Lead Optimisation,
4. Addressing the low preclinical-to-clinical success rate.




[AI_in_pharma]: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1359644621005043
